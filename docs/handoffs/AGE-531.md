# AGE-531 Handoff

## Status

Implemented manual trade tickets, local journal entries, realized-profit
tracking, configurable pay-yourself estimates, and tax-helper JSON export on
branch
`dylanmccavitt2015/age-531-0607-manual-trade-tickets-journal-and-realized-profit`.

The app now generates manual checklist tickets from selected profit-lock
scenarios and trade setups. Tickets include symbol, action, estimated shares,
estimated cash raised or spent, estimated realized gain, tax reserve estimate,
reason, invalidation, checklist copy, and the manual/no-automation disclosure.

The journal panel supports planned, executed, mistake, and result entries.
Entries are local browser-session rows with editable realized P/L, reserve,
pay-yourself amount, journal notes, and tax-prep notes. The realized-profit
summary excludes planned entries, aggregates executed/mistake/result rows, and
uses a configurable pay-yourself rule that defaults to 5% of net realized
trading profit after reserve. Export creates JSON for tax-helper review only.

No brokerage credentials, Robinhood integration, order execution, automated
trading, filing document, or tax-advice surface was added.
